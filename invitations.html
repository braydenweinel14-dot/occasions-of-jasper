BEFORE THE FIRST CONVERSATION

Bring the idea.
Gather the details.

01

Your celebration

Event date, location, type of occasion, and your ideal mailing or handout date.

02

Your paper list

Estimate invitations by households. Note any additional pieces you want to ask about, such as information cards or programs.

03

Your style & budget

Bring colors, wording, reference ideas, and a budget range. Ask what can be created within it.

Ask before you approve.

  • What design, printing, and paper options are currently available?
  • What is included in the quote, and are there minimum quantities?
  • How do proofs, changes, and final approval work?
  • Who checks names, dates, addresses, and spelling?
  • What payment and collection arrangements apply?

Confirm the final proof and production schedule directly with the shop. This website does not provide prices or accept orders.
