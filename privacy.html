Privacy on this website.

This is an independent design concept, not an owner-approved official business website. This notice describes this site’s features; the business and external services have their own data practices.

Messages, orders & payments

This site has no message delivery, checkout, booking, or payment system. Calling, messaging the shop through Facebook, and purchasing from the shop are separate activities.

Your celebration planner

The planner processes your entries in the browser. It does not upload them to a database or save them across page visits. Copying notes places them on your device clipboard. Avoid including sensitive information.

Display preferences

Larger-text and contrast choices are stored in your browser’s local storage under occasions-display. Use Reset display or clear the site’s browser data to remove them.

Analytics, hosting & external services

No Google Analytics or advertising pixels are installed. GitHub Pages may process technical request information to serve the site. The illustrations, styles, and scripts are hosted with this website. The Google map connects to Google only after you select Load Google map. Social sharing and external links connect to the selected provider.

Accessibility

This site includes keyboard navigation, visible focus, a skip link, descriptive image text, labelled form fields, reduced-motion support, and display controls. Browser zoom also works. These features do not constitute a formal accessibility certification.

For access to the physical shop or another communication method, contact the business directly.
